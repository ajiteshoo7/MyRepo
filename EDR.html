Top EDR Tools and Their SIEM Capabilities

A comparison of leading Endpoint Detection and Response solutions and their add-on SIEM/XDR features.

Key Takeaways

  • Most top-tier EDR vendors have expanded their platforms to include SIEM-like features, often branded as **XDR (Extended Detection and Response)** or **Next-Gen SIEM**.
  • These advanced log ingestion and correlation capabilities are **not** part of the standard EDR license and require an additional, separate purchase.
  • The extra cost is typically based on data ingestion volume (per GB) or by the number of endpoints/users, and is often priced through custom quotes.
